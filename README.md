# AI-driven Optimization Techniques in Smart Hospitality Management

This repository contains the SurVis literature browser developed for the literature review coursework of COMP4037 Research Methods.

## Research Topic

AI-driven Optimization Techniques in Smart Hospitality Management

## Research Scope

This literature review focuses on the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and optimization techniques in smart hospitality management, including:

- Smart hotel systems
- Revenue and pricing optimization
- AI customer service
- Service robots
- Predictive analytics
- Decision-support systems

## SurVis Literature Browser

The literature browser contains selected research papers related to AI and optimization applications in hospitality management.
